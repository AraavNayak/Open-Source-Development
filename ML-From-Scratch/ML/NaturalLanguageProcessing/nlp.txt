Natural Language Processing - NLP

Allows computers to understand natural language (usually written, can be spoken)
Goal: turn complex sentences into structured mathematical format
*Context and Attention are very important here! Eg. distinguishing between 'bank' in 'I went to the bank to deposit money' and 'I sat on the river bank'

Intuition:
- break sentences down into pieces "tokens" (words or sub-words)
    "The cube sat" -> ["The", "cube", "sat]
- Word embeddings "Map"
    Instead of giving a word a random ID number, represent each word as a list of coordinates in a multi-dim space

TAKEAWAY: multi-dim coordinates allow the computer to calculate mathematical relationships and similarities between different words.




NLTK -> excels at understanding how a sentence is built
